What it allows is what one can think of as being post-faith, past the need to define oneself for outer audiences. There is no longer any need to be an a-theist, or a interdenominational christian, or a Jew for Jesus. There is instead a constant companionship which opens up heavenly vistas for exploring here on Earth, now, in real time every moment until Death.

Do you remember that time you felt more in Love than ever, or that time you were blown away in Awe by the mountain or the way the clouds reminded your heart of the purest of possibilities. Do you remember when layers and patterns seem to meld and coalesce almost like a symphony, all the threads of our life interweaving into this precious mosaic that felt so "right", so sublime, as if divinity was allowing you to sip from its cup for the merest eternal moment?

That's what this "experience" of Christ is like; *It's* like that all the time, constantly, without interruption for that is *it's* nature. My nature is to be fallible, and forgetful of remembering how incredible it already, and always Is. i stumble in my attempts to stay grounded with Earth each day, pure in my heart, humble before Christ. But i am able to cultivate glimpses, most days! Most days i feel at one with Creation, in union with Grace, a full part of All Love, and it has come through devoting myself to experiential Christhood.

Matters of faith are like talking about how good last night's dinner tasted; it's impossible, and not too rewarding unless the drive is to move towards perfection, but as with food it is not in the talking or thinking about, its' in the partaking, the plunging into with one's whole being the fullness of the moment. It's being able to separate upon your tongue the melodies of the good fats of the stew, behold the subtle interplay, almost like a tango, of the two main spices, neither vying for supremacy but each lost in the dance towards the absolute most magnificent this moment can be.

So too with Christ, and Jesus was a being who fully entered Christhood, through his purity, and this he tried to explain our way. be like the little children, awed and innocent, and all Creation is yours forever. All that is good can be known by each of us, and each of us too can choose to boldly surrender to Love, enter Christhood, and merge with Divinity. Mere talking about it, is, well a tasteless recanting of what we were thinking moments or years ago. Far better to practice becoming Christ (though in its moment it is instananous), practice compassion so that our light will attract others on the surest journey back Home, through (and with) Love.

Practice gratitude knowing you'll never perfect it, until that one moment of re-union with all is One and we see ourselves to be the true beings of Love we already are.
