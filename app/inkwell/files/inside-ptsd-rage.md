###inflicted
Rage inflicted—that is, rage felt, received, experienced on the inside by assault—is nasty. It hurts, first, and always. That's the point after all. It hurts in flesh, and diminishes—by design—whatever it contacts with. Rage exists in vast quantity, and even to word it gives it life. but, we shine the light of Love upon it, and it dims itself. It's that simple. All Love is more powerful for rage is an absence.


###bottom line

That's the bottom line, the criterion that will be used on the last day to determine how much bargaining power you have going into the next life—if that is what you so desire.
You fuck up in this life—hurt people and such—you almost have to come back to make things right. That malign energy will be fulfilled one way or another, so not evading, so come back to life and face your failings.
Better yet, start now, and be Nice.
