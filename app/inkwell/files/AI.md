On Ai, I think humans adapt to the tools available. Yes, we’ve lost the ability to easily read a map, should all the internet maps go down, but it’s not the most challenging skill ever. We could re-learn it quickly. If we had to start writing again by pen and paper, yes we would be hampered from people having gotten rusty at the craft of even simple pen-personship, we’d be set back a few months for re-learning. 
What I count as the current gain, as compared to possible harms, is that I was curious how much longer Earth has lived than the average one of us. I asked AI, sipped my coffee, then read the result, plus explanation. Now I could have spent the 2 minutes looking up via search engines this data, and calculate it myself, or I could have spent 25 olden days minutes finding the correct two encyclopedia entries, sharpening my pencil, and noodled it all out. The tools change, we adapt, that is today’s thought.
