Take the attractive force of gravity. Every since inception it is as if the truest center of the universe, the source of the eruption of matter and energy, has been calling all back to Source.

Electrons, on the other hand, have no mass, but still exist, like thoughts of Creator darting about seeing and seeking relationship with fellow elements, with exotics, in new combinations and familiar stable ones.

Now behold people, and what we do for desire! Every creative expression, every thwarted need every played out as rage or calamitous despair has been borne along by the energy of desire, as if we get swept up into currents at times far too strong for us to live through well, and so we flail and sometimes inflict harm. There’s vast spectras of misdeed, some of which are vile and even evil, many of which harm and cause trauma. It is a field of study I have spent more than 20 years studying, more if you count the time simply being inside ptsd, and all of ptsd seems to distill to people being taken often deathly far away from the general current of human evolution.
By this I mean that there is a path that humans follow, the stages of our lives. Where oak trees go from acorn to tiny sprout to sapling to grow up tree to mature spreading ancestral oak over the course of time and years and seasons, so to do human have before them at birth a path, a way of development that is strong (not impossibly rigid) but pretty locked in. No babies are born sexually mature, say, nor do any 6 month olds suffer from the aches and pains and maladies of old age. Everything has it’s time and there is real difference between a randy gangly 12 year old and a woman starting menopause, just as there is uniqueness between… it’s easy to fill in the different comparisons which taken together give us an oft told tale of birth and childhood and puberty and schooling and house-holding and family and aging and senescence and death. Is there a human story that takes place outside all of this? Very few if any.
Which is to say that trauma, then, is most harmful in two ways. One, the simple destruction of tissue through poisoning or bombing or assault or starvation—all the ways of the cruel. This demolition diminishes not only the present tense vitality of individuals—often with a huge accompaniment of physical pain—it causes an increase of need by the injured, which leaves for community work for the un-injured and well-bodied. A society can absorb some level of traumas, but should we live in a time of massive collective trauma, which perhaps we do, the need will overwhelm and threaten the viability of the unmolested and community will fray deeply. It has happened before.
In addition, there is mental pain, disrupted nervous system activities, an over predominance of stress states, cortisol-flooded panic times which flip into dealt-like depressions, and then back in varying degrees across multiple spectra.
This mental and physical toll is together aspect one.
The second is that as certain developmental milestones are not met, due to survival mode outcompeting all other life activities, a person begins to run a maturity and life-skills deficit. Again, when this is low-level and fairly evenly spread throughout society, society adapts and people carry on just fine, with free time for creative acts of beauty and genius.
But, when many people are missing many milestones, we end up with miscreants pretending to be worthy as politicians when really most national “leader” are stunted people who were harmed over and again at younger ages and missed normal phase of childhood development, not learning to share, for example, or be kind (which is realer than being nice which some of them can pretend to be).
