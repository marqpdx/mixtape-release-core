How do we get there?

	1.	buy local. Know the provenance of every item you buy, and strive daily to buy local. Support small businesses where you can understand what impact your purchase will have on the rest of Creation. (It’s a big task, but these are big days and nights we get to live in).
	2.	buy less, make more. Repair broken things, or find someone nearby to fix it. Reuse everything possible. Be highly suspect of “disposable” anything. 
	3.	want less. If you want a lot now, this might be a bit of work to pull off, but I know from personal experience that I can want more than is necessary. Wanting less is possible; it’s a practice, and I’ll help with that where I can, but yeah, try wanting less.
	4.	want different. Want the world to be just; want there to be more love, more trust and compassion everywhere. Just do. 
	5.	change your focus. This follows from wanting less. Where you place your moment by moment attention is everything. This is where we are choosing our life. This is the supreme chance we get to be fully, actively, vitally alive. We’re encouraged by product-sellers and other hucksters to give this up, to be despairing, but that is folly. Be amazing, be your already brilliant self, care more, love harder. Just do.
	6.	change your expectations. Require yourself to do better. No matter your circumstance, you, I can always do better. To make demanding that of yourself a lasting pattern and part of your continual existence, we have to do the work.
	7.	explore and study yourself. You must persevere with great curiosity in the task of coming to know yourself. You are so vast and untapped, so rich with possibility that it is only through ignorance, fear, and folly that we fail to explore the inner parts of our being.
