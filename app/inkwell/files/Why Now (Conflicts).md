It is apparent to nearly everyone that whatever the cause, whatever the nature of the worldwide reactions, we are amidst an unprecedented pandemic. Whether the scourge of the virus or the associated overflow of fear is more destructive, time will tell. Vigilance is required, change is mandatory. 
Why now, b/c everything is in turmoil. Why now, b/c there are always windows of opportunities with cracks in the paradigms emerge. Cracks are where the light streams in and all our “resilience-building” and grounding and collective community building as been so that we can rise to this occasion. We knew it was coming, something untoward. We all know we’ve been living beyond our sustainable earthly means. Who can think driving a car or flying in a jet is sustainable? Who can think eating foods shipped across thousands of miles of ocean was better than eating food from over the hill.
