###details matter

i could probably start every essay w/ that sub-head: details matter. yeah they do.

they way do.

for example, i remember trying to make sense of it all as a volunteer medic at the berkeley free clinic, early 80's, right as AIDS was busting out of its cosmic hibernation. i was 20, and taught people how to do primary care for people who had no other choice, or who valued our attentive service. we were supervised by a doctor of course, and we saw a lot of scabies and fungus and rashes. so it is.
