This seems to rely on the notion that Jesus only exists as an externality, that is something to be believed. This neglects what i know of as the experiential view, which transcends belief because it emerges from experience, rather than faith. 
I have had experiences where Jesus, avatar of Love in simplest of terms, has entered by being during moments of darkness, and brought me immediate relief. For me, Jesus the Christ is a living scion of Source, of Love, of whatever begat us and brought us to life. I need not "believe" anything, b/c i know, just as i do not need to believe in gravity, for i have experienced it daily.
Faith is a weak foundation, b/c, as this article demonstrates, it's fickle, and worse, it's susceptible to human interpretation, which leads to demagoguery and all forms of tyranny.
One of the best accounts of this, for those who like to read about this as part of the path of learning, is the work of Paramhansa Yogananda, especially "The Second Coming of Christ: The Resurrection of the Christ Within You" which talks extensively about the capacity within each of us to have direct experience of Jesus as a Christ'ed being, and how each of us can become "like God" through allowing and inviting this energy of Love into our own beings. This removes the need to rely on other people to "guide our faith" and instead empowers us to be magnificent beings of Love and compassion, just as Jesus taught.
