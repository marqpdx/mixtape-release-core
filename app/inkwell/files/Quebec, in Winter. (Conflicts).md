Part of this work is coming to know yourself, through study and grace and kindness. It’s part of the requirement of living well and fully on Earth. Problem is, we’ve lived in such messy, unclean times, that there is much darkness accumulated in our collective and singular psyches. There is much ugliness to behold, and ultimately befriend, no matter how impossible that seems.
