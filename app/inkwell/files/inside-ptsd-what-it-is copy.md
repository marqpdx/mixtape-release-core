### What is PTSD?

It's a disordered state. It is a near continual line of dis-ease strecthing across day, months, years. Like some people dealing with diabetes or alchoholism or fibromyalgia, it sometimes never goes away. People live w/ PTSD forever.

That means they are basically never calm, usually scared, often uptight, near panicked, tired, unfocused, and vague, without the joyfulness of knowing Awe, Beauty, and even Love.
