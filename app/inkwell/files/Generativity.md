Doing stuff can the plunging in to adventure side of life, even on the mundanest of days (I’m a huge fan of the mundane), but there’s more. There’s the effect that our doing has upon ourselves, Earth, and other living beings, and that needs be accounted for, I direct myself.
Our doing causes effects in the world, and this is a form of creativity. We are co-creating reality each moment, together and alone, whether we are aware or not. 
The way I use this information is to realize that it’s extremely important for me to have maximal knowledge of myself, and given my own experience with this continual adventure, it will at times require courage, b/c me and my nervous system have truly been scared at times, and that cannot be blithely dismissed in light of this information.
What I find fascinating here is that on one hand healing takes time, always, whether from accidents or violence, and yet also healing is available to each of us instantaneously, all the time. This is too complicated a thought to explain quickly, so I will attempt to bring this notion into greater light as I type.
